# README

## Overview

This visualization explores how **secondary-school progression** relates to a country's economic prosperity and living conditions using data from the **World Development Indicators (WDI)** dataset published by the World Bank.

The story is presented through four scenes that gradually build on one another. It begins by introducing differences in educational opportunity across countries, then examines how education is associated with GDP per capita and the percentage of people living in urban slums. These relationships are presented as **associations rather than evidence of causation**. The final scene allows viewers to explore additional development indicators themselves, encouraging them to identify patterns and exceptions within the data.

## Scene 1 – Educational Opportunity Around the World

**Question:**  
How does secondary-school progression vary across different countries?

This introductory scene displays a dot plot showing the percentage of students progressing to secondary school for each country. It establishes the wide variation in educational opportunity around the world and identifies countries with particularly high and low progression rates.

**Interaction:**
- Select a different **year**.
- Filter by **region**.
- Hover over a country to view its exact values.
- Click a country to keep it highlighted.
- Click **Next Scene** to continue the narrative.

## Scene 2 – Education and Economic Prosperity

**Question:**  
Do countries with higher secondary-school progression also tend to have higher GDP per capita?

This scatterplot compares educational progression with GDP per capita. As countries move farther to the right, educational progression increases, while countries higher on the chart have greater GDP per capita. The overall upward trend suggests a positive relationship between education and economic prosperity.

**Interaction:**
- Change the **year**.
- Filter by **region**.
- Hover over a country to view detailed information.
- Click a country to highlight it.
- Use the **Next** and **Previous** buttons to move between scenes.

## Scene 3 – Education and Living Conditions

**Question:**  
How is educational progression related to the percentage of people living in urban slums?

This scatterplot compares educational progression with the percentage of the urban population living in slums. Countries with higher educational progression generally appear lower on the chart, indicating smaller slum populations. The visualization suggests that stronger educational outcomes are often associated with improved living conditions.

**Interaction:**
- Change the **year**.
- Filter by **region**.
- Hover over countries to view their values.
- Click a country to highlight it.
- Continue using the **Next** and **Previous** buttons to navigate.

## Scene 4 – Explore the Relationship Yourself

**Question:**  
How does secondary-school progression relate to other development indicators?

The final scene allows viewers to explore additional relationships by comparing secondary-school progression with GDP per capita, unemployment, poverty, or population living in slums. Rather than presenting a single conclusion, this scene encourages users to investigate the data for themselves and recognize that development is influenced by many factors.

**Interaction:**
- Change the **year**.
- Filter by **region**.
- Select a different **development indicator** from the outcome dropdown.
- Hover over countries to view detailed information.
- Click countries to compare individual observations.
- Use the **Reset** button to restore the default view.
